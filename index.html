Orion's Belt Team Poker League Team League · Two-Month Cycles · Three Divisions · Once a Week

Orion's Belt is a team-based poker league inspired by the three stars in Orion's Belt — Alnitak, Alnilam, and Mintaka. You play as an individual while competing on a team with separate tables, similar to the Replay Team Championship but running all year long. Seasons run in two-month cycles across three rotating divisions. Organizers handle all scheduling — just show up once a week and play.

Early Birds Team League Team League · Private · Daily Midday Games · 5,000 Chip Entry

Early Birds is a team-based private league with daily midday games in both MTT and cutthroat formats. Five game types rotate through the week, with each scored session paired with a fast-paced cutthroat event. Monthly Members Appreciation games are open to all with no maximum player count and an added prize pool.
